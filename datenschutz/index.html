5. YouTube (Click-to-Load)

This website embeds YouTube videos using a "click-to-load" (façade) approach. No connection to YouTube is made until you explicitly click the play button on a video. After clicking, the video is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com — YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which does not set tracking cookies by default. Once you click play, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
